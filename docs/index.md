# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2025-01-01

Shockwave Calculator is a mobile app focused on gas dynamics calculations. The app currently operates fully offline.

## Summary
- **No accounts required:** You can use the app without creating an account.
- **No personal data collected:** We do not collect, store, or share personal data.
- **On-device processing:** All calculations are performed locally on your device using the inputs you provide.

## Data Collection and Use
Shockwave Calculator does not collect, transmit, store, or share personal data. The app does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking.

## Future Features
We may add optional connectivity features in the future (for example, downloading updated reference or data tables). If we introduce online features, we will update this privacy policy and the Google Play Data Safety disclosures before release.
